# Store Listing — PustakaHadith

## Description (English)

**PustakaHadith** — Your complete offline hadith reference app.

Browse, search, and study hadith from 9 major collections (Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah, Ahmad, Darimi, Malik, Nasa'i) with Arabic text, transliteration, and translations in Malay, Indonesian, and English.

**Key Features:**
- 62,169 hadith records across 9 collections
- Full-text search in Arabic, Malay, and English
- AI-powered meaning search (runs 100% offline on your device)
- Three-panel reading view: Arabic + Transliteration | Malay | Indonesian | English
- Dark/Aqua/Neutral themes with adjustable font sizes
- Bookmarks to save your favourite hadith
- Share hadith via WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, or Instagram
- Text-to-Speech for hadith reading
- Jump to any hadith by number (e.g. "Bukhari 433")

**100% Offline** — No internet required after first launch. All data stays on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no ads.

**Privacy-first** — Zero data collection. Your bookmarks and settings never leave your device.
